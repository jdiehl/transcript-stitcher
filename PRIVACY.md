# Privacy Policy

Transcript Stitcher processes all data locally on your device.

## What the App Does

- Monitors the macOS clipboard while monitoring is active
- Detects text that resembles transcript content
- Merges overlapping fragments into a single assembled transcript

## Data Storage

- No data is stored persistently
- No data is sent over the network
- No data is shared with third parties
- Closing the app discards all captured content

## Clipboard Access

While monitoring is active, the app reads text from the system clipboard. Only text that matches transcript-like patterns is processed. Non-transcript clipboard content is ignored.
