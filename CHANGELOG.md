# Release 1.1

- **Undo/Redo** — Made a mistake? Easily undo or redo changes to your transcript.
- **Color-coded chunks** — Each pasted section is now visually distinct, making it easier to follow the flow of your transcript.
- **Window Restoration** — The app remembers where you left off when you reopen it.
- **Improved performance** — Faster stitching and smoother scrolling, even with long transcripts.
- **Better font & layout** — Updated to the system font with an improved window design.
- **Smarter clipboard monitoring** — The app now pauses monitoring while you're actively using it, and picks back up when you return.

# Release 1.0

- **Transcript stitching** — Automatically combines clipboard content into a single, continuous transcript.
- **Start/stop controls** — Easily control when the app monitors your clipboard, with convenient keyboard shortcuts.
- **Monitoring bar** — Visual indicator shows when the app is actively monitoring your clipboard.
- **Background processing** — Stitching runs off the main thread for smooth performance.
